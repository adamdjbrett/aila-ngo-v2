# Come Gather for the AILA Harvest Art & Craft Market

**Saturday, November 21, 2026 · 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM**
**Skä•noñh – Great Law of Peace Center, 6680 Onondaga Lake Parkway, Liverpool, NY 13088**

As the leaves come down along Onondaga Lake and the season turns toward winter, the American Indian Law Alliance (AILA) invites you to come and join us for the **AILA Harvest Art & Craft Market**. For one full Saturday, the Skä•noñh Center will be filled with the work of local Native craft vendors and artists, with traditional food, music, and raffles throughout the day.

## Why a Harvest Market

Harvest is a time to give thanks for what the earth has provided and for the people whose hands and knowledge carry traditions forward. A market of Native makers is one of the most direct ways to honor both. When you buy beadwork, basketry, jewelry, art, or other handmade pieces from the person who made them, you support artists, families, and the living cultural knowledge their work holds.

This market is also a chance to visit together. Come to shop, come to eat, come to listen to music, and come to spend time with neighbors in a place dedicated to peace and wellness.

## The Place

The market takes place at the Skä•noñh – Great Law of Peace Center on the shore of Onondaga Lake. *Skä•noñh* is an Onondaga greeting meaning "peace and wellness," and the center tells the history and values of the Haudenosaunee from the perspective of the Onondaga Nation, the spiritual and political center of the Six Nation Confederacy ([Onondaga Historical Association](https://www.cnyhistory.org/about/who-what-we-are/); [Maxwell School, Syracuse University](https://www.maxwell.syr.edu/news/article/we-are-not-a-people-of-the-past-tenth-decade-project-builds-ties-with-indigenous-community)). It is a fitting home for a gathering that celebrates Native artists and community.

## What to Expect

- **Local Native craft vendors and artists** offering handmade work, just in time for holiday gift giving
- **Traditional food** to enjoy on site
- **Music** throughout the day
- **Raffles**, so bring a little extra and try your luck

## Plan Your Visit

- **When:** Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Where:** Skä•noñh – Great Law of Peace Center, 6680 Onondaga Lake Parkway, Liverpool, NY 13088 ([Onondaga Historical Association](https://www.cnyhistory.org/programs/contact/))
- **More information:** Call (315) 447-4840 or visit [aila.ngo](https://www.aila.ngo)

Many Native artists work in cash, so consider bringing some along with your cards.

## About AILA

The American Indian Law Alliance is an Indigenous women-led organization working for sovereignty, land return, and the protection of Mother Earth. Its work runs from international Indigenous advocacy at the United Nations to public education and direct community care here in Central New York. Gatherings like the Harvest Market are part of that same work: making space for Native people, culture, and economy to thrive.

Please come and join us, and bring your family and friends. We look forward to seeing you on November 21.
