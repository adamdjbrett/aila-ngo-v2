We are glad to be part of Pride Day at the Skä•noñh: Great Law of Peace Center. Come join us on **Saturday, September 19, from 12 to 4pm**. All 2SLGBTQIA+ people and allies of all ages are welcome. This is a family friendly event.

[![Pride Day at Skä•noñh event details](/assets/images/content/PrideDay_EventDetails2.png)](/assets/images/content/PrideDay_EventDetails2.png)

## Where and how much

The Skä•noñh Center sits on Onondaga Lake at 6680 Onondaga Lake Pkwy, Liverpool, NY. Parking is free and on site.

Entry is sliding scale, $0 to $20. Syracuse University students get in free with a student ID, sponsored by the SU Department of Religion.

While you are there, visit the cultural center and learn the story of the Onondaga, the People of the Hills, and of all the nations of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, which was formed on the shores of sacred Onondaga Lake.

## Schedule

- **12:00** Words Before All Else, the welcoming words
- **12:30** Traditional Haudenosaunee food provided by Tina Thomas (Onondaga Nation, Eel Clan). Vegetarian and gluten-free options. Free food until it is gone.
- **1:30** Performance by the Onondaga Women's Singing Society
- **2:00** Panel of Two-Spirit and Indigenous queer community members
- **3:00** Guided tour of the Skä•noñh: Great Law of Peace Center

The menu: venison BBQ sliders, corn soup with scoons, Three Sisters skewers, mush with berries, yellow cornbread with strawberry, strawberry drink, and tea.

[![Pride Day at Skä•noñh menu and community partners](/assets/images/content/PrideDay_EventDetails3.png)](/assets/images/content/PrideDay_EventDetails3.png)

## Accessibility

The building and bathrooms are ADA compliant, with an elevator and stairs. Bathrooms are all gender. This is a masked event. Air purifiers will be running, and free masks are provided by Better Breathing Bloc.

## Partners

Pride Day is presented in partnership by the [Skä•noñh Great Law of Peace Center](https://www.instagram.com/skanonhcenter), the [American Indian Law Alliance](https://www.instagram.com/americanindianlawalliance), [2Spirit Sanctuary](https://www.instagram.com/2spirit.sanctuary), [Meet Cute](https://www.instagram.com/meet.cute.events), the [SU Department of Religion](https://www.instagram.com/religionsyracuse), the [Indigenous Values Initiative](https://www.instagram.com/indigenousvalues), and [Better Breathing Bloc](https://www.instagram.com/betterbreathingbloc). Learn more about each partner on their websites and social media.

We hope to see you at the lake.
